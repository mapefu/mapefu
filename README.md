## ⭐ Featured project

### [Beating the Human at Flappy Bird](https://github.com/FerrerJan/Treball-de-Recerca-IA)

<a href="https://github.com/FerrerJan/Treball-de-Recerca-IA">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FerrerJan/Treball-de-Recerca-IA/main/docs/img/gameplay.png" alt="APFLY-IA playing Flappy Bird" align="left" height="150">
</a>

This was our high-school research project, built together with [@FerrerJan](https://github.com/FerrerJan) and [@teo-clerk](https://github.com/teo-clerk). We wrote **APFLY-IA**, a NEAT-style neuroevolution AI, from scratch in GDScript inside our own Godot version of Flappy Bird. Then we studied how population size, inputs and mutation types affect how fast it learns, across **3,798 training runs**.

**Main result:** learning follows a logarithmic curve, and with the right setup the AI plays perfectly from generation ~8.
